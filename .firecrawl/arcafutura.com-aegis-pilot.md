AEGIS Stability Pilot Brief

# Measure the risk before the governor governs.

Plug AEGIS into one real training workflow without initially allowing it to alter training. The pilot establishes signal behavior, operational fit, and qualifying-incident exposure, then helps the training team choose the response policy that fits its stack.

[Discuss a pilot](mailto:founder@arcafutura.com?subject=AEGIS%20stability%20pilot) [Review the evidence](https://www.arcafutura.com/aegis/evidence/)

Two phases

## Start passive. Earn the right to intervene.

Phase 1 · Shadow mode

### No training changes

- Instrument one agreed training workload.
- Record candidate alerts without changing optimizer behavior.
- Measure false alerts, monitoring overhead, and operational compatibility.
- Estimate the customer's actual restart, rollback, and recovery exposure.
- Keep customer weights and data inside the customer environment.

Phase 2 · Operator-selected response

### Choose how the stack responds

- Select alert-only monitoring, checkpoint, quarantine, halt, adaptive braking, or a staged combination.
- Fix authorization requirements, intervention limits, and automatic disengagement rules.
- Compare the selected policy against a matched shadow or replay control.
- Keep operator override and final control of the run with the customer.

Predeclared outcomes

## Define containment value before the run begins.

- Detection timing for agreed qualifying incidents.
- False-alert and false-intervention rate on clean and difficult-but-valid training.
- Downstream validation damage with and without bounded control.
- Monitoring and intervention overhead.
- Checkpoint, rollback, restart, and engineer-response exposure.
- Any missed incident, regression, or operational failure.

Design partner contributes

### Workload and incident economics

One training workload, compute access inside its environment, one ML engineering contact, and customer-owned estimates for restart, rollback, recovery, and interruption costs.

Arca Futura contributes

### Integration and evidence

Integration support, protocol design, shadow monitoring, containment analysis, and a final evidence report. No removal of weights or proprietary training data.

**Commercial boundary.**

AEGIS savings are conditional on a qualifying damaging event occurring and on bounded containment avoiding some restart, rollback, or recovery exposure. The pilot measures those terms in the customer's environment; it does not assume a universal savings percentage.
