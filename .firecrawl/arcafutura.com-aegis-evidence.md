## What this proves—and what it doesn’t.

AEGIS shows that real-time monitoring, authorization, and bounded response can work as one system. The operator chooses how that system responds.

Prospective matched studies support early detection and reduction of downstream optimizer damage for a registered high-loss disturbance class across Qwen and Llama, with transfer through Qwen3-4B.

- AEGIS does not detect or repair every harmful data problem.
- It cannot make a permanently bad stream safe.
- Severe disturbances can saturate the current bounded actuator.
- Reliable protection from recurrent disturbances is not established.
- Results through 4B do not establish frontier-scale or distributed-training generalization.
- The calculator is a customer-input avoided-loss scenario, not a measured savings multiplier.
- Historical time-to-target studies remain research context, not the primary product claim.
