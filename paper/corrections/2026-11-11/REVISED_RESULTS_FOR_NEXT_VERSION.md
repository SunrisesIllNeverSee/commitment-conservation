## Suggested replacement section (markdown source)

### Archived prototype experiment and metrics

To assess whether a proposed compression–extraction–reconstruction gate preserves deontic commitments through repeated transformation, a single ten-step run was conducted on **20 canonical signal categories** with three regimes: unmediated paraphrase, compression only, and compression plus commitment extraction/reconstruction. The model was **GPT-4o-mini** (see `working/runs_archive/2026-03-17/convergence_v2_report_234059.md`, Methodology) and the frozen full trace is `convergence_v2_234059.json`, SHA-256 `746aa4ec7066b1231dde7e06e7d0f2fa7bd59274c28ec1dcc5131077e8a6df19`. This is a prototype convenience sample with one execution per condition and repeated measurements on the same signals, not 200 independent observations or a multi-model inference study.

The primary archived semantic proxy, labeled **NLI**, is the arithmetic mean of two yes/no entailment judgments from a language model, taken against a canonical reference generated by the harness's extractor/reconstructor. Its allowable scores are **0, 0.5 and 1**. It is **not a calibrated entailment probability or an independent semantic ground truth**. A second metric, lexical `stability`, compares extracted commitment-bearing word sets by **Jaccard intersection-over-union**, which penalizes legitimate paraphrase as well as content loss. Blank values for the definition and rule categories were **excluded rather than imputed**.

| At recursion depth 10 | Gate mean ± sample SD | Baseline mean ± sample SD | n |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Binary-judgment entailment proxy | **0.775 ± 0.343** | **0.875 ± 0.222** | 20 |
| Lexical word-set Jaccard | **0.333 ± 0.355** | **0.464 ± 0.363** | 18 |

For the archived semantic proxy the paired gate–baseline mean difference was **−0.100**, comprising **three gate improvements, eleven ties and six declines**. Seven *qualitative failure mechanisms* were independently identified for the gate: stripping legal-consent conditions, directive semantic collapse, first-person reconstruction of an imperative, subject/modal loss, negation/modality weakening, urgent-time dilution, and an omitted procedural ordering prerequisite (internal July 8 correction note). Six negative paired scores and seven qualitative cases are **different categorizations**; procedural can be semantically wrong despite a tied coarse NLI bucket.

The prior V.05 Table 2 row of **0.94 ± 0.03 gated / 0.42 ± 0.12 baseline** does **not** reproduce as Jaccard or semantic NLI on this frozen trace. Its related figures, **92% vs 38% identity preservation**, **0.006 vs 0.058 drift rates**, and reported correlation `r = 0.89, p < 0.001` require independently identified archived analyses or must be removed. The hard-coded curve in `fig_L1_plot.tex` does not match the linked raw data at iteration ten; it should be regenerated from this source with a visible denominator per point, or withheld.

These observations show that the **particular tested gate failed to deliver a positive aggregate advantage**. They do not invalidate the question of whether some independently specified, governed transformations can preserve commitments, but **do not demonstrate a general conservation law**. The authors propose improved actor, modality, condition, exception, temporal, order and authorized-amendment handling, followed by **preregistered** tests against plain rule pinning, registry reattachment and endpoint authorization, with blind independent annotation and downstream behavioral controls. All improved numbers must come from new, fully disclosed experiments; the proposed improvements are not observed results.

### Interpretive boundary

- Do not silently reclassify all negative gate cases as scoring artifacts. The archived original/gate outputs show substantive errors in the seven identified cases.
- Do not use **13 favorable signals** to replace the complete 20-signal cohort.
- Do not equate a statistical trend of source-word overlap with a mathematical conservation law.
- Do not assert generalization to finance, code or mathematical proofs from this single run.
- Keep DOI/v05 source intact and issue a traceable new version only after review.
